maggie/submissions

What is MAGGIE?

MAGGIE is a printed magazine for writing that sounds like someone wrote it. Emails sent as journal entries, a review that admits it changed its mind, a conversation that started in a car. We like work that is personal, a little unhinged, and honest about where it came from.

We are scared of our language being read as AI, so we want the parts that couldn't be anything else: the typo you kept, the opinion you'd defend at dinner, the month you'd call a bitch. Have a point of view. Changing your mind halfway through counts as one.

Pen names are fine. Tell us if you'd rather not be named.

What do the categories mean?

Reviews
Anything that can be reviewed: photography, music, film, products, events, services, a show, a shop, a sandwich. Say what it is, what it did to you and whether it was worth it. Enthusiastic or sceptical, never in between. 350–900 words. Photos you took are welcome.
Journal
Diaries, notes-app entries, emails, letters, replies to your post. The running account of a life, dated if you can. Any length, from one line to a month. Screenshots and scans are fine; we will typeset or keep them as they are.
Photos
A series from one place or one stretch of time: a city, a trip, a room, a season. 6–20 images at full resolution (JPG or TIFF), with the place and the date. Short video is welcome for the website.
Conversations
Two or more people talking: in a car, on the phone, over a drink, about anything. Record it (with everyone's yes), transcribe it, cut the boring bits. 1,500–4,000 words, plus a title and the date it happened.
Music
Writing about a song, an album or a night out, or a playlist with a line about each track. 8–20 songs for a playlist; up to 1,000 words for writing. Send links, not files.
Essays
Arguments about culture, aesthetics, technology, fashion, celebrity and why we feel the way we do about them. Talks you have given count. 1,000–3,000 words, with your sources at the end.
Horoscopes
A run of weekly horoscopes for one season: 6–12 weeks, 100–200 words each. Real astrology or not, we won't ask.

How can you send something?

Email info@meanwhilegettingmeaner.com with the category in the subject line and the work attached, or a pitch of a few lines if it isn't written yet. Word, Google Docs, PDF or the body of the email are all fine. Send big photo sets as a shared link.

You keep the rights to your work. We ask to print it in MAGGIE and show it on this site. Everything we receive gets a reply from a person, even if it isn't a yes this time.
