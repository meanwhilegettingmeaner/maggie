Koons' Baloon Dog has been something I've encountered in furniture shops, "Galleries", and it had been an object of desire for a certain type of people I've known in my life.

This is the kind of person who is into new money, luxury goods. Fast money culture. It has been something I'm opposed to. But as I was experiencing his latest show Porcelain Series at Gagosian, I was able to understand his process and his way of thinking through material, scale and references more.
