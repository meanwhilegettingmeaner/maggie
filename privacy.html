MAGGIE is a printed magazine and this is its website. This page explains what the site keeps about you, which is very little.

What the site remembers

Your browser keeps a few small settings so the site works the way you left it. They stay on your device and are not sent to us:

  • the content size you picked with the size button (40–100);
  • whether you have already seen the opening screen and the newsletter pop-up during this visit.

Orders

When you buy a copy, payment and your shipping address are handled by Stripe. We receive your name, email and shipping address so we can send your copy; we never see your full card details. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to what they collect.

Newsletter

If you sign up, we use your email address only to send you MAGGIE news. You can ask to be removed at any time by writing to info@meanwhilegettingmeaner.com.

Other services the site uses

  • Weather: the date line shows the weather near you. The site guesses your city from your computer's time zone (no location prompt) and asks open-meteo.com for the forecast.
  • Music: song previews are looked up on Apple's iTunes search. Links open Apple Music or Spotify.
  • Fonts: some type is loaded from Google Fonts.

Those services may see your IP address when your browser contacts them, under their own privacy policies.

Contact
